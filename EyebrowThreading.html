Precision Threading & Brow Studio in Mortlake, Richmond

Experience the most precise and skin-friendly method of facial hair removal at Athena Clinical Spa in Mortlake, Richmond. Threading is an ancient technique that uses a loop of 100% organic cotton thread to trap and lift hair directly from the follicle — no heat, no chemicals, no tearing of the skin. Unlike waxing, threading only catches the hair, making it the safest option for clients using Retinol, chemical peels, or acne medications.

Our threading specialists are masters of geometric brow architecture, creating incredibly crisp, clean lines that define your natural arch and lift the entire face. Enjoy slower, finer hair regrowth and a completely smooth, irritation-free complexion after every visit. Complement your threading with our eyebrow and lash tinting, Henna Brows, or the Nouveau LVL Lash Lift for a fully polished, defined look.

We recommend every 3 to 4 weeks to maintain the architectural shape of the brows. Because threading removes hair from the root, regrowth is finer and slower than shaving or depilatory creams.
Most clients describe a mild stinging sensation — far less intense than waxing. Because no skin is pulled, there is no post-treatment redness or peeling. We apply a soothing aloe vera gel afterwards to calm the skin.
Waxing adheres to both the hair and the skin, which can strip the top layer of the epidermis. Threading uses a fine cotton thread that only wraps around the hair shaft and lifts it from the follicle, leaving the surrounding skin completely undisturbed. This makes threading the superior choice for precise shaping and sensitive skin types.
Threading is generally safe for those using topical Retinol, as the thread does not contact or remove skin. However, if you are taking oral Accutane (Isotretinoin), we advise waiting until your course is complete before any facial hair removal.
